# World Destroyers: Series Plan

Follow-up Shorts and a long-form essay, built from the [60-second production kit](Fictions_Deadliest_Higgsfield_Kit.md).

**Theme:** World Destroyers. Characters who destroy planets, civilizations, universes or timelines, judged on what they destroyed, how they did it, and how far it reached. That gives Eren, Paul, Galactus, Zeno and Anti-Monitor distinct roles without pretending their death tolls are directly comparable.

### BlavkMist test: three Shorts

Keep posting stream clips alongside these. Start with topics close to the games already on the channel, then widen:

1. "Could we survive the Rumbling?"
2. "How deadly is Kratos compared with fiction's biggest killers?" or "Resident Evil's monsters are terrifying. These fictional threats are worse."
3. "Who Is Fiction's Greatest World Destroyer?": the full comparison from the kit.

Judge the test on watch time, comments and subscribers gained, not just views. One upload won't settle it, but three will tell you something real. Keep Higgsfield spending small until you know you enjoy making these and viewers ask for more.

## Candidate characters and hooks

| Character or threat | Short's hook | Why it works |
|---------------------|--------------|--------------|
| The Qu, *All Tomorrows* | "These aliens didn't just defeat humanity. They redesigned it." | They genetically alter humanity's descendants. Better as a disturbing transformation story than as a death counter. |
| Tyranids, *Warhammer 40,000* | "For this alien swarm, your civilization is just food." | Their living fleets consume biomass and leave worlds stripped of life. Strong imagery, simple premise. |
| Photino Birds, *Xeelee Sequence* | "What if the universe became uninhabitable because another species preferred it that way?" | A cosmic conflict over the conditions different forms of life need. Much stranger than an evil emperor. |
| AM, Harlan Ellison's story | "It killed almost everyone. The survivors got something worse." | A compact story about hatred, extinction and endless suffering. |
| The Crimson King, Stephen King | "This villain wanted to destroy the structure holding reality together." | Lets you explain the Dark Tower and separate attempted destruction from actual deaths. |
| Paul Atreides, *Dune* | "The promised savior became the symbol of a war that killed 61 billion." | A clearly stated total, plus a question of responsibility. |

## Shorts to make if the test works

These pick up after the three test Shorts above.

1. **"Who Is Fiction's Greatest World Destroyer?"**: the existing script. A broad introduction to the series.
2. **"The aliens that turned humanity into something else"**: the Qu. Start with familiar humans, then reveal their altered descendants.
3. **"Who was worse: Eren or Paul Atreides?"**: compare motives, responsibility and consequences. Keep each world's population separate.
4. **"The AI that left five people alive"**: AM alone. An empty world and five lights can carry most of the video.
5. **"Powerful doesn't mean the highest death toll"**: Pennywise and the Crimson King versus characters with documented mass casualties. A twist on the original list.

**After the test:** if the BlavkMist Shorts do well, try AM and the Qu next. They show whether viewers prefer comparisons, psychological horror or strange alien lore before you spend credits on a full series.

## Long-form video for Among the Billions: "When Destroying a Planet Isn't Enough"

Aim for 6–8 minutes. The sections climb in reach, and each one answers a different question.

| Section | Reach | Examples | Main question |
|---------|-------|----------|---------------|
| War and leadership | Civilizations | Darrow, Paul | How do we count destruction caused through orders? |
| Extermination | A world's people | Eren, AM | What separates mass killing from ending a world? |
| Consumption | Planets | Galactus, Tyranids | Does destroying worlds to survive change how we judge them? |
| Transformation | A species | The Qu | Is remaking a species another way of destroying its world? |
| Erasing reality | Timelines, universes | Zeno, Anti-Monitor | What does destruction mean when whole realities disappear? |
| Closing comparison | All of them | All of them | Who destroyed the most, and who caused the worst fate? |

**Keeping credits down:** make the long video an illustrated essay with narration, stills, diagrams and slow editor zooms. Reuse the three animated clips from the first Short, and cut each later Short from its matching section. A longer runtime doesn't require more generated footage.
