# BlavkMist Explores: Series Plan

Commentary videos on BlavkMist, posted alongside stream clips under the label **"BlavkMist Explores"**. The goal is to cover fiction broadly: anime, games, comics, horror and books. Dragon Ball stays in the mix without taking over.

## What changed

The first plan was a 60-second list: name, destruction, number, next character. It delivered facts without suspense or a surprising idea, so it's been replaced. Every video now needs an angle, a reveal, or a question the viewer wants answered. Spending more on visuals won't fix a weak story, so stories come before generation.

- The [60-second list kit](Fictions_Deadliest_Higgsfield_Kit.md) is kept for its prompts and sources, but it's no longer the plan.
- The other six stills from that kit are **paused**.
- The Rumbling still already generated goes to the "Could We Survive It?" Rumbling video.

## Long-form master: "Who Is Fiction's Worst Destroyer?"

Full package: [Worst_Destroyer_Master_Script.md](Worst_Destroyer_Master_Script.md). About 11–12 minutes, 16:9.

The angle: the biggest body count and the worst destroyer can be different winners. The first draft ended with three verdicts: Paul for a stated death toll, Anti-Monitor for destructive reach, AM for cruelty. Ten Shorts can be cut from its chapters after the master is reviewed.

### Ranked version: 10-to-1 countdown

The master has been rewritten as a countdown, kept in `World_Destroyers_Complete_Master.docx` (not yet in this repo). It ranks **demonstrated destructive scale**, with confirmed body counts shown alongside. The cruelty comparison is the ending twist.

Places 4–6 are editorial and debatable because their lifetime totals are unknown. The Qu and Stephen King's creatures stay in the bonus section.

## First World Destroyers Short: Eren → Galactus → Anti-Monitor

Three franchises and three distinct visuals: humanity trampled, planets consumed, universes erased. Each reveal makes the previous scale feel small. Labels must keep one difference clear: Eren destroys a civilization, Galactus destroys planets, Anti-Monitor destroys universes.

This replaced a Frieza → Zeno → Anti-Monitor version, which had two Dragon Ball characters out of three and felt like a Dragon Ball video.

Its source material is in the master script (chapters 2, 5 and 8). It doesn't have its own 60-second script yet.

## Judging the test

Judge the BlavkMist Explores videos on watch time, comments and subscribers gained, not just views. One upload won't settle it, but three will tell you something real. Keep Higgsfield spending small until you know you enjoy making these and viewers ask for more.
