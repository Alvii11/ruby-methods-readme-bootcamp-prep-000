# BlavkMist Explores: Series Plan

Commentary videos on BlavkMist, posted alongside stream clips under the label **"BlavkMist Explores"**. The goal is to cover fiction broadly: anime, games, comics, horror and books. Dragon Ball stays in the mix without taking over.

## What changed

The first plan was a 60-second list: name, destruction, number, next character. It delivered facts without suspense or a surprising idea, so it's been replaced. Every video now needs an angle, a reveal, or a question the viewer wants answered. Spending more on visuals won't fix a weak story, so stories come before generation.

- The [60-second list kit](Fictions_Deadliest_Higgsfield_Kit.md) is kept for its prompts and sources, but it's no longer the plan.
- The other six stills from that kit are **paused**.
- The vertical Rumbling still already generated is kept for Shorts, such as the "Could We Survive It?" Rumbling video. The 16:9 master has its own landscape Rumbling frame.

## Long-form master: "Fiction's Greatest Destroyers, Ranked"

**Current master and current priority.** Full package: [Greatest_Destroyers_Ranked_Master.md](Greatest_Destroyers_Ranked_Master.md). About 10–11 minutes, 16:9. Build the complete countdown first; cut Shorts afterwards.

Order: 10 Eren → 9 AM → 8 Frieza → 7 Paul → 6 Tyranids → 5 Reapers → 4 Galactus → 3 Thanos → 2 Zeno → 1 Anti-Monitor. Ranked by demonstrated destructive scale, with confirmed body counts shown and uncertain placements labeled. It ends with a separate cruelty verdict. The Qu and Stephen King's creatures are in the bonus section.

The unranked "Who Is Fiction's Worst Destroyer?" package ([Worst_Destroyer_Master_Script.md](Worst_Destroyer_Master_Script.md)) is replaced. It's kept only for its storyboard prompts and source links.

## Possible later cut: Eren → Galactus → Anti-Monitor

Not the immediate priority. Once the master exists, this Short can be cut from #10, #4 and #1. Three franchises and three distinct visuals: humanity trampled, planets consumed, universes erased. Labels must keep one difference clear: Eren destroys a civilization, Galactus destroys planets, Anti-Monitor destroys universes.

## Judging the test

Judge the BlavkMist Explores videos on watch time, comments and subscribers gained, not just views. One upload won't settle it, but three will tell you something real. Keep Higgsfield spending small until you know you enjoy making these and viewers ask for more.
